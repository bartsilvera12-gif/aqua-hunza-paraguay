Why Clean Water Is Essential for Cattle & Buffalo Health

Water is life — not just for humans but for cattle and buffaloes too.

← Volver al blog

Cattle and buffaloes require clean, fresh water daily for health, growth and milk production. While farmers prioritise quality fodder and supplements, water quality is frequently overlooked as a simple yet effective improvement strategy.

Why water is essential for cattle and buffaloes

Milk production

Lactating animals consume 40–60 litres daily, increasing to 80–100 litres during peak lactation. For every 1 litre of milk produced, cows require approximately 4–5 litres of water.

Digestion and feed efficiency

Water facilitates the breakdown of fibrous feed. Cattle consuming clean water show a 15% increase in feed intake compared to those drinking dirty water.

Temperature regulation

Water maintains body temperature through sweating and respiration, preventing heat stress during hot seasons.

Growth and weight gain

Young animals need adequate hydration for development. Dehydration can lead to a 10–20% reduction in growth rate.

Effects of dirty or contaminated water

  • Disease transmission via waterborne pathogens.
  • Reduced feed intake and growth (20–30% weight gain reduction).
  • Lower milk yield (1–2 litres daily reduction).
  • Toxicity from pesticides and heavy metals.
  • Parasitic infections from stagnant water.

How to ensure clean water

  • Clean troughs weekly using brushes.
  • Provide fresh water daily.
  • Ensure proper drainage around water sources.
  • Use covered or elevated tanks.
  • Filter or treat water if necessary.
  • Prevent access to stagnant water.
  • Install automatic waterers for large farms.

Economic benefits

Clean water provision yields higher milk yields, better weight gain, reduced veterinary costs and improved reproduction. Water is an affordable, valuable farm resource: simple maintenance practices maximise animal welfare, productivity and farm profitability.
